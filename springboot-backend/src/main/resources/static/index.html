Frequently Asked Questions

How do I determine the right size for me? ▼

Every product detail page has an interactive "Size Guide" button displaying standard bust, waist, and hip measurements in both inches and centimeters. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

What are the shipping delivery times and costs? ▼

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days nationwide. All orders totaling $75 or more qualify for FREE delivery automatically at checkout.

What is your return and exchange policy? ▼

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return and exchange window. Simply contact our support team and return items unworn with original tags attached for a full refund or exchange.
